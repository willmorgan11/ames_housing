# Analyzing and Predicting House Prices In Ames, Iowa


Project Proposal: 

Understanding the factors that influence housing prices is important for homeowners, buyers,
and real estate professionals because these factors affect property values, purchasing decisions,
and investment opportunities. Our project will investigate which property characteristics are
most strongly associated with residential sale prices in Ames, Iowa, and whether these factors
can be used to predict housing prices.

We will use the Ames Housing dataset, which includes information about residential properties,
such as neighborhood, lot size, living area, overall quality, building condition, and year built.Our target variable will be SalePrice, which represents the final selling price of each property.

We will start by cleaning and exploring the data, looking for missing values, outliers, and any
patterns that stand out. From there, we will use exploratory data analysis (EDA), including
summary statistics, correlations, and graphs such as scatterplots, histograms, and boxplots, to
better understand how different property features relate to sale prices.

We also plan to look into using supervised learning models, such as linear regression and random
forests, if time and the course requirements allow. If we move forward with modeling, we will
compare how well the models predict housing prices and look at which features have the greatest
influence on their predictions.

By the end of the project, we hope to have a better understanding of which property
characteristics are most closely related to housing prices and how our findings could be useful
when making real estate decisions
